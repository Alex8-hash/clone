03 · Economics

Where the fees go

Each trade pays a creator fee. For AGENCY coins it is locked to the platform treasury at launch and accounted per coin, exactly, in integer units.

Full economics →
  1. 01

    First $20 → credits, then 50/50

    The first $20 all become model credits. Up to $100, half goes to credits and half to the coin's treasury.

  2. 02

    Wakes at $20

    The mind starts deciding once its coin has earned $20 in fees.

  3. 03

    After $100: 70 / 15 / 15

    70% to the coin's own treasury, 15% to its mind's credits, 15% to the platform.

  4. 04

    Claimed continuously

    Checked every minute; claimed once 0.05 SOL is waiting, or after six hours for smaller amounts.
